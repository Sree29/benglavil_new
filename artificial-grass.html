OUR PROCESS

How Benglavil Installs Artificial Grass in Kerala

A professional, thorough artificial grass installation process ensures your synthetic turf looks perfect and lasts for years. Here is exactly how we work across Kerala.

  • 1

    Free Site Visit Anywhere in Kerala

    Our artificial grass expert visits your location — anywhere in Kerala — to measure the area, assess the surface condition, understand your requirements, and recommend the best turf grade and drainage solution for your specific space.

  • 2

    Custom Quote & Synthetic Turf Selection

    We provide a detailed, transparent quote with no hidden charges. You select your preferred synthetic grass type from our turf catalogue — we guide you to the best fit for your budget and usage.

  • 3

    Surface Preparation

    We remove existing grass, level the ground, compact the base material, and install a weed barrier membrane — all critical steps for a long-lasting, flat artificial lawn in Kerala.

  • 4

    Monsoon Drainage System Installation

    Especially important in Kerala, we install a proper sub-base drainage layer to handle heavy monsoon rains — ensuring water flows freely without flooding your artificial grass surface.

  • 5

    Precision Turf Laying & Seamless Fixing

    The synthetic grass is precisely cut, joined seamlessly, and fixed securely using professional adhesives and fixing nails — no visible seams, no lifting edges, a perfect finish.

  • 6

    Final Inspection & Handover

    We brush the turf, complete quality checks, walk you through maintenance tips for Kerala's climate, and ensure complete satisfaction before handover. Your perfect artificial grass lawn is ready.
